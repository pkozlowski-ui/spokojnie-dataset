# spokojnie – open dataset / otwarty zbiór danych

**EN** · [PL poniżej](#pl)

Published answers of [spokojnierodzice.pl](https://spokojnierodzice.pl) ("spokojnie – czy to normalne?"): is it normal at this age? For each question: a short answer, the typical range (norm), when to worry, and when to see a doctor or call 112 (Poland, children 0–5 years). Language of the data: Polish.

- **Non-commercial, solo, community project.** No ads, no affiliate links, no paid features.
- **Source rule.** Every claim is written in our own words and linked to a named public source (NHS, WHO, Polish ministries and agencies, and others), with a verbatim quote of at most one sentence. No source in the registry, no claim.
- **Only published records.** This export is generated by the same tool as the website and contains exactly the records the site publishes. Nothing else is included: no drafts, no review notes.
- **Export date:** 2026-10-30 (`generated_on` in `records.json`). **Records:** 261. **Sources:** 12.
- **Files:** `records.json` (data), `records.schema.json` (JSON Schema of every field), `LICENSE.md` (licences, attribution), `CITATION.cff`.
- The same file is served at https://spokojnierodzice.pl/data/records.json. This repository is a snapshot; the website is the source of truth.

## Licences (per claim, not one for the whole file)

Each claim, myth and short answer has a `license` field: the licence of its **strictest source** (`CC-BY-4.0`, `CC-BY-SA-4.0`, `CC-BY-NC-4.0`, `CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0`). Reuse each record as a whole under all licences it lists, and do not drop the "SOR / 112" and "Do lekarza" urgency panels: removing them changes the medical meaning. Verbatim quotes are not covered by our licences. Details and attribution rules: [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). No source endorses this project.

## How to cite

See [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff). Short form: *spokojnie – czy to normalne? Open dataset, spokojnierodzice.pl, 2026-10-30, https://github.com/pkozlowski-ui/spokojnie-dataset*. Link to the question page (`url`) when you show an answer.

## Not medical advice

This is not medical advice. It describes the typical course in a population of children and does not assess any individual child. In an emergency call **112**.

## Report an error

Found a mistake in a record? Write to **bledy@spokojnierodzice.pl** with the question id (for example `Q001`). A reported medical error stops promotion of the project until the sources are reviewed and the record is corrected.
